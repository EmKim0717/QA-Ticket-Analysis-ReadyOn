Zendesk QA Rubric 3.3 — Support Quality Framework (Guide Only)

Qualitative evaluation reference & coaching guide. Used by AI Co-Analyst for automated auditing & feedback.

1. Problem Understanding & Relevance

Deep understanding including context, urgency, and user type (Associate/Manager/Internal). Anticipate actual needs.

2. Completeness & Resolution Ownership

All questions answered with clear next steps. Full ownership taken. Propose a call after 5 touches without progress.

3. Accuracy & Product Knowledge

Information correct and aligned with product behavior. Explains clearly without exposing internal system jargon.

4. Troubleshooting Quality & Depth

Proper investigation before escalating. Never rely solely on AI or assumptions. Include logs, links, screenshots, reproduction steps.

5. Meaningful Communication

No empty responses. Before closing, must include a genuine, specific check-in referencing actual issue.

6. Structure, Tone & Clarity

Short paragraphs, blank line before signature every time, acknowledge patience if user waited.

7. Jira Reporting Quality

Bug/FR filed correctly with full repro steps & evidence. Internal notes match Jira. (N/A if no Jira filed).

8. Internal Notes Quality

Required structure: Issue Description / WW Item & Links / Troubleshooting & Actions / Next Actions / Docs Used. Link Engineering follow-ups.

9. Process Adherence & Operational Excellence

Never share OTP/credentials outside standard flow. Correct Stop-The-Clock macro based on who filed (EP vs Client). Correct subject format: 123456 - Subject

Non-Negotiables (⚑ Critical Red Flags)

  • ⚑ Generic or irrelevant responses ignoring user query
  • ⚑ No troubleshooting before filing Jira
  • ⚑ Misuse of macros (check-in macro instead of real reply)
  • ⚑ Ignoring user questions
  • ⚑ Poor/missing internal notes
  • ⚑ Incorrect or misleading info provided
  • ⚑ No patience acknowledgement after delay
  • ⚑ Requester not updated to actual client on EP-filed ticket
  • ⚑ Wall of text / missing blank line before signature
  • ⚑ Generic closing check-in without actual issue reference
  • ⚑ Replied without reading full history on handed-over ticket
  • ⚑ OTP or credential shared via call/chat/channel (CRITICAL)
  • ⚑ Engineering follow-up not linked back via internal note

Quality Warnings (⚠ Secondary Flags)

  • ⚠ Direct reference to FullStory or internal monitoring tools
  • ⚠ Unredacted internal tools/links visible in shared screenshots
  • ⚠ Used word "bug" instead of "issue" or "unexpected behaviour"
  • ⚠ Wrong stop-the-clock macro used on EP-filed tickets

Key Communication Directives

  • Empower, don't do it for them: Guide clients with instructions.
  • Match greeting to stage: No "Thank you for reaching out" on follow-ups! Use "Thank you for the update".
  • Always use client's first name: Never use "Hi there".
  • Acknowledge before moving forward: "Thank you for sharing that" before next steps.
  • Soften deletion language: Use "removed" or "no longer visible", never "deleted".

Stop-the-Clock Macro Guidelines

EP Filed on Client's Behalf: Use macro "ALL | Ack and stop the clock - looped in". Then change Requester field to actual client, add original EP requester to CC, insert client's first name in greeting.

Quality Incident Notification (QIN)

Formal internal document issued when an interaction involves process violations outside standard rubric levels. Not disciplinary—used for structured coaching.

Quality Dispute Submission Process

  1. Submit Dispute Form within 3 business days.
  2. Assigned to QA SME (24–48 hours SLA).
  3. Decision: Approved OR Escalated to Leadership.
