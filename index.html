ITSM Best Practices & Zendesk QA SOP Benchmark

Combines ITIL Service Desk Quality Assurance Standards with internal SOP guidelines. Used by the AI Co-Analyst for automated auditing.

1. Triage & Categorization

Ensure accurate classification between Incidents (service interruption) and Service Requests. Verify impact & urgency ratings align with SLA targets.

2. Service Level Management

Proactive management of First Response Time (FRT) and Resolution SLAs. Pending states must be appropriately managed to avoid idle customer tickets.

3. Knowledge-Centered Service (KCS)

Agents must leverage knowledge base articles, log clear troubleshooting steps, and flag missing documentation for Continual Service Improvement (CSI).

4. End-to-End Ownership & FCR

Drive First Contact Resolution where possible. Avoid unnecessary ping-pong transitions between tiers without actionable troubleshooting evidence.

5. Governance & Security

Strict verification of user identity before performing access changes. Sensitive data, passwords, or credentials must never be shared in plain text.

6. Customer Centricity

Empathetic, clear, and professional tone tailored to end-user technical maturity. Set explicit next steps and clear expectation timeframes.

1. Problem Understanding & Relevance

Deep understanding including context, urgency, and user type. Anticipate actual customer needs.

2. Completeness & Resolution Ownership

All questions answered with clear next steps. Propose a call after 5 touches without progress.

3. Accuracy & Product Knowledge

Information correct and aligned with product behavior. Explains clearly without exposing internal jargon.

4. Troubleshooting Quality & Depth

Proper investigation before escalating. Never rely solely on AI or assumptions. Include logs, links, reproduction steps.

5. Meaningful Communication

No empty responses. Include a genuine, specific check-in referencing actual issue before standard closure.

6. Internal Notes Quality

Required structure: Issue Description / Links / Actions Taken / Next Actions. Link Engineering follow-ups.

Critical Non-Negotiables (⚑)

  • ⚑ Generic or irrelevant responses ignoring user query
  • ⚑ Escalating without basic troubleshooting
  • ⚑ Misuse of macros or generic sign-offs
  • ⚑ Poor or missing internal notes
  • ⚑ Incorrect or misleading technical info
  • ⚑ OTP / credentials shared directly in plain text

Key Communication Directives

  • Empower, don't do it for them: Guide clients with instructions.
  • Match greeting to stage: No "Thank you for reaching out" on follow-ups! Use "Thank you for the update".
  • Always use client's first name: Never use "Hi there".
  • Acknowledge before moving forward: "Thank you for sharing that" before next steps.
